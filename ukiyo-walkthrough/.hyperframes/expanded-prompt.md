# Ukiyo 25s Product Walkthrough

Style: premium dark SaaS using #071018, #0e1a24, #f4efe6, #f0b35a, #48d6c2, #6ee7a8. Typography uses Avenir Next/system UI for product narrative and IBM Plex Mono/Consolas for operational data.

Rhythm: slow-build-BUILD-PEAK-breathe-CTA across five scenes in 25 seconds.

Global rules: Captured Ukiyo screens are framed as live product evidence. Each scene uses ambient glow, grid/scanline depth, operational labels, and a persistent business-flow rail. Primary transition is blur crossfade/push; the final beat resolves with success glow and fade to black.

## Scene 1: Customer Login, 0-4s

Concept: The viewer enters the product through a secure customer account. The login capture sits inside an elevated browser frame while the workflow rail marks authentication as the first event.

Choreography: Browser frame rises, login screen sharpens, security label types on, first workflow node activates.

Transition: Blur crossfade into catalog.

## Scene 2: Product Browsing, 4-9s

Concept: The storefront becomes the customer decision surface. Catalog and product detail captures are layered like a product analyst zooming into intent.

Choreography: Catalog slides left, product detail card cascades forward, SKU telemetry counts in.

Transition: Push/blur into purchase flow.

## Scene 3: Purchase Flow, 9-15s

Concept: Customer intent becomes a transaction. Cart and checkout/success capture are connected by a glowing order pipeline.

Choreography: Cart locks in, checkout rail fills, success toast pulses, order ID appears.

Transition: Cinematic dashboard reveal.

## Scene 4: Success Confirmation, 15-19s

Concept: Confirmation is the customer-facing proof that the system accepted the order.

Choreography: Success indicator expands, notification card floats, operational handoff marker arms.

Transition: Whip push into admin.

## Scene 5: Admin Dashboard, 19-25s

Concept: The customer's action instantly impacts business operations. The admin order dashboard appears with KPIs and order-management overlays.

Choreography: Admin screenshot lands, new order row highlights, KPI cards count up, final thesis line resolves.

Transition: Fade to black in final second.
